Different types of molecules – genes, proteins, and DNA regions – are interacting and coordinating to carry out cellular functions. However, in reality, we can only capture partial and static views of cellular profiles. Our lab aims to develop deep learning methods that leverage large-scale public data to predict multimodal views of each cell and characterize the relationships between different types of molecules in cellular regulation.
